07 · PRIVACY + LIMITSMd Mahafujul Alam

Show what the system

Data protection

Raw IDs are hashed before clean Silver output; public case counts 1–4 are suppressed.

Not anonymity

Linked totals and views can still create inference risk. Bronze and quarantine stay restricted.

Not deployment-ready

Synthetic data, very low detection precision, a simple forecast baseline, and no live inventory integration.
