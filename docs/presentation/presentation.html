07 / PRIVACY + LIMITSWalid Talal

What the prototype cannot prove

Synthetic evidence

Encounters, facility stock, and outbreak labels are synthetic; they cannot establish real-world accuracy.

Disclosure risk

Clean Silver uses a salted hash. Weekly counts 1-4 are suppressed, but linked totals may still reveal information.

Operational limits

Very low precision, a fixed forecast, no live inventory input, and unresolved historical metrics.

Alerts are review prompts. A real pilot needs authorized data, privacy review, domain oversight, and human approval.
Protect identifiers · show uncertainty · retain human review08 / 09
